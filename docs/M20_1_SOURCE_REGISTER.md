# M20.1 — Source Register

Access date for every web source: **2026-10-11**. “Fact” means the linked source directly documents the statement. “Inference” is the M20.1 interpretation. Pricing and account eligibility were not queried through authenticated pages; unknowns remain `UNKNOWN`.

The Binance Spot WebSocket, REST and filter documents were rechecked on **2026-10-11** for M20.1R. In the current documented payloads, raw trade has `E` and `T`; diff-depth has `E`, `U` and `u`; bookTicker has `u` but no `E`/`T`; REST depth snapshot has `lastUpdateId`. Diff-depth update speed is 100 ms or 1000 ms, and the snapshot is capped at 5,000 levels per side. These are documented facts. Cross-stream global ordering, intra-batch trajectory, queue position, client receive timestamp and exact one-way latency are not documented fields/guarantees.
