## External primary sources

| ID | Source | Documented fact used | Project inference or unresolved point |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | [Binance public-data repository](https://github.com/binance/binance-public-data) | Binance publishes daily/monthly Spot and Futures files, schemas, download examples and checksums; daily files are normally available the next day. It warns that archived files can later be corrected. | Batch acquisition and resumption are technically possible. Exact coverage and gaps for a proposed pair/data type still require a non-economic probe. |
| S2 | [Binance Vision Dataset Terms v1.0](https://github.com/binance/binance-public-data/blob/master/TERMS_AND_CONDITIONS.md) | Vision datasets are free under stated non-commercial terms for personal historical backtesting; the terms restrict live proprietary execution and govern derivatives of the datasets. | Vision archives cannot be assumed suitable for the project's eventual live-trading objective. M21.1 therefore does not select them as the authoritative experiment input. Legal applicability is not decided here. |
| S3 | [Binance USD-M market-data API: funding history](https://developers.binance.com/en/docs/catalog/core-trading-derivatives-trading-usd-s-m-futures/api/rest-api/market-data) | `GET /fapi/v1/fundingRate` exposes `symbol`, realized `fundingRate`, `fundingTime`, associated `markPrice` and `rateType`; results are ascending, accept start/end time, and are capped at 1,000 rows with an IP rate limit. | Timestamp pagination can be resumed locally. The page states no complete-history guarantee, revision history or earliest row per symbol. |
| S4 | [Binance USD-M market-data API: premium-index klines](https://developers.binance.com/en/docs/catalog/core-trading-derivatives-trading-usd-s-m-futures/api/rest-api/market-data) | `GET /fapi/v1/premiumIndexKlines` exposes timestamped OHLC premium-index bars at intervals including 1h, with start/end time and a 1,500-row maximum. | A completed 1h premium bar can be joined after its close. Exact historical retention and correction policy are not stated. |
| S5 | [Binance Spot market-data API: klines](https://developers.binance.com/en/docs/catalog/core-trading-spot-trading/api/rest-api/market) | `GET /api/v3/klines` returns UTC-addressable Spot OHLCV, quote volume, trade count and taker-buy volumes, with start/end time and a 1,000-row maximum. | It supports resumable historical acquisition without a continuous connection. A bar open is not an executable quote, so explicit slippage remains necessary. |
| S6 | [Binance developer introduction](https://developers.binance.com/en/docs/introduction) | Binance describes its APIs as supporting historical market data, trading systems and bots. | This supports technical compatibility, but does not resolve regional product eligibility or all contractual questions for a specific user. |
| S7 | [He, Manela, Ross and von Wachter, “Fundamentals of Perpetual Futures”](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/Delivery.cfm/4301150.pdf?abstractid=4301150&mirid=1) | The paper models recurring funding as the mechanism anchoring a perpetual to its underlying and documents economically meaningful deviations under trading frictions. | Funding and premium are economically distinct observables from Spot OHLCV. The paper does not establish a profitable long-only Spot timing rule. |
| S8 | [He, Wang and Zhang, “A Shared Template Without Shared Feedback”](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/Delivery.cfm/6185958.pdf?abstractid=6185958&mirid=1&type=2) | The 2026 working paper models constrained arbitrage and reports that basis reversals are associated with directional liquidation and that capped rates can coexist with persistent gaps. | This motivates a falsifiable crowding/reversal hypothesis. It is recent working-paper evidence and does not prove out-of-sample Spot returns. |
| S9 | [Baur et al., “Bitcoin time-of-day, day-of-week and month-of-year effects”](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.frl.2019.04.023) | Across seven exchanges the study found time-specific return anomalies but no persistent return pattern, while volume seasonality was more persistent. | A directional calendar strategy has weak mechanism-level support and a large implicit multiple-testing space. |
| S10 | [Glassnode API introduction](https://docs.glassnode.com/basic-api/api) and [pricing](https://studio.glassnode.com/pricing?billingCycle=yearly) | Historical API access requires a Professional subscription/add-on; plan history, resolution and point-in-time access differ. | Exact cost is not public for the required professional configuration, and account/API-key acquisition is outside M21.1. |
| S11 | [Glassnode exchange-metric documentation](https://docs.glassnode.com/basic-api/endpoints/distribution) | Exchange metrics use address labels and statistical methods that are updated; ordinary series, especially recent points, can change over time. | Immutable point-in-time data would be required for a leakage-safe exchange-flow test. Ordinary reconstructed histories are unsuitable as if they had been known unchanged at time T. |
| S12 | [“Return-forecasting and Volatility-forecasting Power of On-chain Activities”](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.06327) | The primary study reports that intraday USDT exchange net inflows forecast BTC and ETH returns most strongly at a one-hour frequency. | This establishes a testable literature claim, not its replication on the project's venue, costs or long-only implementation. |

## Source limits

The Binance API documents specify schemas and request limits, not guaranteed full historical coverage, immutable revisions, delisted-symbol completeness or a monetary service-level commitment. The Glassnode pages document product capabilities, but the required Professional quote and exact metric entitlements are unknown. No source establishes statistical power for the experiment proposed below.
