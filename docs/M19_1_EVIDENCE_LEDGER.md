# M19.1 — Evidence Ledger

## Scope and interpretation

This ledger records what the saved project evidence supports and why further unsupported tuning of completed lines is frozen. Engineering maturity is not evidence of alpha. Predictive discrimination, cross-sectional ranking quality, and realizable trading edge are separate claims: improvement in one does not establish either of the others.

The 2023–2025 market history and January–September 2026 M18.2 period have been viewed. They remain usable for explicitly labeled exploratory analysis, but after a hypothesis is selected they cannot be presented as a new independent Test.

## Project conclusion

Further unsupported tuning of the completed technical-classification, ranking/filter/gate, generic news-feature, adaptation, and Donchian lines is closed. This does not equate weak predictive signal, weak ranking signal, and absent trading edge; each conclusion retains its stated scope. M18.2 is a frozen research baseline, not a deployable strategy.

Missing data blocks the three M19.1 designs; it does not disprove their economic mechanisms. A later project would first need a separate decision on data feasibility, cost, provenance, and realistic execution, followed by a preregistered question. Existing viewed periods may support exploratory work but cannot become a new independent Test after hypothesis selection.
